depth — walk to unlock · last updated september 17, 2026

short version

the core loop — which apps you track, how deep you are, and your steps — is meant to stay on your phone. we don’t sell your data. we don’t run ads. we don’t track you across other companies’ apps.

what stays on your device

  • screen time / family controls: you pick apps in apple’s picker. depth uses that selection to estimate scrolling time and, when you set it up that way, to shield those apps until you walk back. depth does not read the content inside those apps.
  • motion / steps: step counts (and related motion data you allow) climb you back toward the surface and unlock shielded apps.
  • widgets & live activities: show depth / unlock progress from data already on the device.

accounts

some builds include an optional owner sign-in for admin tooling. if you use it, name and email may be stored (passwords hashed) only to run that account — not for ads or tracking. app store builds are intended to work without that local owner gate.

purchases

subscriptions go through apple. we don’t receive your full card number. apple’s privacy policy covers app store payments. monthly subscription with a short free trial where offered.

analytics & tracking

no third-party advertising sdks. no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

your choices

  • revoke screen time / motion permission in ios settings.
  • cancel a subscription in ios settings → apple id → subscriptions.
  • delete the app to remove on-device app data.
  • for owner-account deletion, email the contact below.

children

depth is not directed at children under 13.
