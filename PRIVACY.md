# Privacy Policy — depth

**Last updated:** September 17, 2026

depth (“the app”) is made by Peter Hough. This policy covers the iOS app **Depth - walk to unlock** (bundle id `com.peterhough.depth`).

## Short version

The core loop — which apps you track, how deep you are, and your steps — is meant to stay on your phone. We don’t sell your data. We don’t run ads. We don’t track you across other companies’ apps.

## What the app does on your device

- **Screen Time / Family Controls:** you pick apps in Apple’s picker. The app and its extensions use that selection to estimate scrolling time and, when you set it up that way, to shield those apps until you walk back. Apple mediates this; depth does not read the content inside those apps.
- **Motion / steps:** step counts (and related motion data you allow) are used to climb back toward the surface and to unlock shielded apps. That processing is for the feature on your device.
- **Widgets & Live Activities:** show your depth / unlock progress using data already on the device / app group.

## Accounts (optional / development)

Some builds include an optional **owner** sign-in for admin tooling. If you use it, we may store:

- name  
- email address  
- account credentials (stored hashed on the server you connect to)

Used only to run that owner account. Not used for advertising or tracking. Production App Store builds are intended to work without that local owner gate.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by **Apple**. We don’t receive your full payment card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store payments. Product: monthly subscription with a short free trial where offered.

## Analytics & tracking

depth does not include third-party advertising SDKs. We do not use your data for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Data leaving the device

- **Default App Store experience:** depth is designed so Screen Time selection and step/depth state stay on your phone (and its App Group / extensions).
- **If you connect an owner backend:** account fields above may be sent to that server over HTTPS or your configured API base URL.
- We don’t sell personal data.

## Your choices

- Revoke Screen Time / motion permission in iOS Settings.
- Cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
- Delete the app to remove on-device app data (standard iOS uninstall).
- For owner-account deletion requests, email the contact below.

## Children

depth is not directed at children under 13. Don’t use the owner account features for a child under 13.
